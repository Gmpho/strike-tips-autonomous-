# Privacy Policy — Strike Tips Racing Bot

**Effective Date:** June 2026  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Jurisdiction:** South Africa (POPIA Compliant)

---

## 1. What This Covers

This Privacy Policy explains how Strike Tips Racing Bot ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you interact with our Telegram bot and web dashboard.

**Critical notice:** This is a **paper-trading only** educational system. No real money is ever wagered, collected, or paid out. All bankroll figures, profit/loss, and ROI metrics are simulated for learning purposes only.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Telegram User ID** — Your numeric Telegram ID (e.g., `5398642046`)
- **Chat ID** — The conversation context where messages are exchanged
- **Messages you send** — Commands, queries, and interactions with the bot
- **PIN entry** — If you use the `BOT_ACCESS_PIN` for authorized access

### 2.2 Information Generated by the System
- **Paper betting history** — Simulated selections, stakes, odds, and outcomes
- **Virtual bankroll snapshots** — Balance, profit/loss, ROI over time (all simulated)
- **Analysis requests** — Races you ask the system to evaluate
- **Learning engine data** — Aggregated, anonymized performance metrics used to improve predictions

### 2.3 Third-Party Data (Processed on Your Behalf)
- **Race data** — Public race cards, odds, and results from TAB4Racing, Betway, and Racing Post
- **AI provider queries** — Your questions are sent to Groq (Llama 3.3 70B) and/or Google (Gemini 2.0 Flash) for analysis. We do not control their data practices.
- **Embedding model** — Queries are embedded via local Ollama (`embeddinggemma:300m`) or Gemini cloud fallback
- **Memory storage** — Conversation context stored in ChromaDB (hosted at `api.trychroma.com`) and Honcho (`hch-v3-...` workspace)

## 4. How We Use Your Data

1. **Provide the service** — Respond to your racing queries, deliver paper-trading alerts, simulate bankroll updates
2. **Improve predictions** — Learning engine analyzes historical paper-trading outcomes to refine edge calculations
3. **Maintain context** — ChromaDB + Honcho memory preserves conversation history across sessions
4. **Compliance & safety** — Enforce 18+ access via PIN, rate limiting, and abuse detection
5. **No marketing** — We do not send promotional messages, sell data, or share with advertisers

## 7. Your Rights (POPIA Section 5)

You have the right to:

1. **Access** — Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
2. **Rectification** — Correct inaccurate data
3. **Deletion** — Request erasure of your data ("right to be forgotten")
4. **Restriction** — Limit how we process your data
5. **Portability** — Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
6. **Object** — Object to processing based on legitimate interest
7. **Complain** — Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa)

**To exercise any right:** Send `/privacy` to the bot or email the administrator. We respond within 30 days per POPIA.

## 8. Security Measures

- **Encryption in transit** — All API calls use HTTPS/TLS
- **Local processing** — Ollama models run on your infrastructure (no data leaves your server for local inference)
- **Access control** — PIN-gated bot access (`BOT_ACCESS_PIN`)
- **Rate limiting** — Prevents abuse and enumeration attacks
- **No plaintext secrets** — API keys stored in `.env`, never in code or logs
- **Audit logging** — All bot interactions logged for compliance review
