# South African Horse Racing Knowledge Bundle

Reference knowledge for South African thoroughbred horse racing — tracks, going conditions, merit ratings, and betting strategies.

Coverage: 7 major racecourses, NHRA merit rating system (introduced 1999), weight-for-age scales, and value-based betting strategies.

## Tracks

- [Kenilworth](tracks/kenilworth.md) — Cape Town, turf, left-handed, dual tracks (new 2800m / old 2700m)
- [Durbanville](tracks/durbanville.md) — Cape Town, turf, left-handed, 2200m, winter venue
- [Fairview](tracks/fairview.md) — Gqeberha (PE), turf & polytrack, right-handed, 2700m
- [Turffontein](tracks/turffontein.md) — Johannesburg, turf, right-handed, steepest climb in SA
- [Vaal](tracks/vaal.md) — Free State/Highveld, turf, right-handed, longest straight in SA (1600m)
- [Scottsville](tracks/scottsville.md) — Pietermaritzburg, turf, right-handed, 2300m oval
- [Greyville](tracks/greyville.md) — Durban, turf & polytrack, right-handed pear-shaped, 2800m
