# Clonmel Racecourse

**Location:** Powerstown Park, Clonmel, County Tipperary, Ireland
**Surface:** Turf (flat + jumps)
**Direction:** Right-handed, galloping with a climb

## Character

Stamina and the ability to climb. Horses that travel but fail to see out the
finish are the recurring hard-luck story. Right-handed jumping/flat track —
course form (either code) transfers better than raw ratings suggest.

## Draw bias (measured since 2014)

- **9f–11f: HIGH draws cost ~1.8L** — strongest measured effect. Avoid wide
  in middle-distance handicaps.
- Rest of the card: no strong bias; race shape dominates.

## Punter rules

- Stamina first: downgrade brilliant milers stretched out, upgrade grinders.
- Middle-distance wide draws are an auto-downgrade, not a tie-breaker.
