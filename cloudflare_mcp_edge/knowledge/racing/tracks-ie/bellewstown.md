# Bellewstown Racecourse

**Location:** Hill of Crockafotha, County Meath, Ireland (racing since 1780)
**Surface:** Turf (flat)
**Direction:** Left-handed, sharp, undulating, ~9f circuit, uphill 3f finish

## Character

Balance exam: cambers, tight turns, road crossings, uphill finish. A horse
that can't hold position through the undulations is beaten before the hill.
Up-on-the-pace helps anything handling the track.

## Draw bias (measured since 2014)

- **5f: HIGH strongly favoured** — dogleg means inside draws run FURTHER
  (start mid-course, run straight). Counter-intuitive, bettable.
- **Mile: slight LOW lean.** Longer: middle-high costly over 12–15f (−2L).
- Front-runners drawn low are a different (better) bet than front-runners
  drawn wide — early position + balance compound.

## Punter rules

- Question horses without balance, early position, or hilltop-course evidence.
- Sprint: want high. Mile: want low. Staying: avoid middle-high.
