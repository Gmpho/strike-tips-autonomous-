# SA Tote Exotic Pools Reference

Source of truth for POOL FACTS is TAB (Computaform leg_info, TAB sheets, TABGOLD
operational policy). This page summarises those rules for ticket construction.
All bets R1 unit; TAB outlet minimum R6, online R1. Fractional betting applies
to Jackpot, Pick 6, Place Accumulator, Trifecta, Quartet and Bipot.

## Multi-race pools (the daily game)

| Pool | Legs | Qualifier per leg | Notes |
|------|------|-------------------|-------|
| Bipot (BI) | 6 | 1st or 2nd | First-timer rule applies (SA races) |
| Place Accumulator (PA) | 7 | 1st, 2nd or 3rd | No first-timer rule |
| Pick 6 (P6) | 6 | Winner | First-timer rule applies; must be won |
| Jackpot / Pick 4 (JP) | 4 | Winner | No first-timer rule; JP1 + JP2 on 9+ race cards |
| Pick 3 | 3 consecutive | Winner | Rolling (R1-3, R2-4…); must be won, any-2 consolation if no all-3 winner |
| Rolling Double | 2 consecutive | Winner | R1-2, R2-3, etc. |

Card-size offerings (TAB standard): 6-race card → P6 R1-6, JP R2-5. 7-race →
PA R1-7, P6 R2-7, JP R3-6. 8-race → BI R1-6, PA R2-8, P6 R3-8, JP R4-7.
9+ race → BI R2-7, PA R3-9, P6 R4-9, JP R5-8 + R6-9.

## Single-race pools (picked races only)

| Pool | Needs | How it wins |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Exacta | 2+ runners | 1st + 2nd in exact order. Single, Perm, Box. |
| Trifecta | 3+ runners | 1st + 2nd + 3rd exact. Single, Multi, Float, Box. Popular: 1 banker for 1st with 3+ for 2nd/3rd. |
| Quartet | 4+ runners | 1st–4th exact. 6 entry types incl. floating/ roving bankers (1–2 floaters must run top-4 any order). |
| Swinger | 2 of first 3 | Any two placed horses (cheapest cover, R2 min online). |

Place-field rules (decide PA/saver logic): 5 or fewer runners = NO place betting;
6–7 runners pay 1st+2nd; 8–15 pay 1st–3rd; 16+ pay 1st–4th.

## When each pool is the right weapon

- **Jackpot**: the daily bread. 4 legs keeps tickets affordable; play both JP1+JP2 on 9+ cards, concentrate on one when the other card half is murky everywhere.
- **Pick 6**: life-changing pools, brutal variance. Only play with fractional cover and a strong banker somewhere; never a full-field perm on a small budget.
- **Bipot**: forgiving (1st OR 2nd) — best pool for competitive cards where winners are hard but the first two are readable.
- **PA**: the grinder's pool (top-3). Seven legs of place = survival game; favour consistency over brilliance.
- **Pick 3**: rolling, cheap, surgical. Use on a 3-race sequence where you have a genuine edge in all three — not as a lottery ticket.
- **Quartet**: chaos tax. Play ONLY big fields (12+) where the favourite is vulnerable; box or float, never straight-line without a very strong read.
- **Trifecta**: medium fields (8–13) with a confident winner — Multi (banker 1st + 3–4 for 2nd/3rd) is the value play.
- **Exacta**: small fields or two-horse duels. Single or Box; the cheapest exact-order edge in racing.
