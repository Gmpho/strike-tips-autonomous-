# Exotic Ticket Construction Doctrine

TAB data tells you WHAT runs. This page tells you HOW to play it. All costs
assume the R1 unit with fractional betting available (Jackpot, Pick 6, PA,
Bipot, Trifecta, Quartet).

## The vocabulary

- **Banker**: one horse per leg you stand on. Dies if it loses (multi-race) or misses the frame slot (single-race).
- **Saver**: extra horses in a leg that keep the ticket alive if the banker fails.
- **Field**: all runners — the emergency parachute, used only when a leg is pure chaos.
- **Permutation cost**: horses_leg1 × horses_leg2 × … × R1 (then fractional %). A 4×4×4×4 Jackpot = 256 perms = R256 full, R4.80 at ~2%.

## The prime directive: widen the murk

A multi-race ticket dies in exactly ONE leg — always the one you covered
thinnest relative to its chaos. So every rand goes to your WEAKEST leg, not
spread evenly:

1. Rank each leg by uncertainty (field size × competitiveness × your read strength).
2. The murkiest leg gets the next saver. Repeat until budget is spent.
3. Never add a 4th saver to a leg while another leg has only a banker in a 14-horse field.

Three straight 1-leg losses (Oct-2026: Fairview/Greyville/Durbanville) all died
the same way — one uncovered horse in one leg. The math: adding ONE horse to ONE
4-coverage leg costs ~25% more and covers exactly this failure mode.

## Concentrate vs spread

Two thin tickets (JP1 + JP2 at R4.80 each) both dying by one leg = R9.60 burned.
One R9.60 ticket with widened murky legs survives where both thins died. Rule:
when both halves of a card are murky, play ONE pool wide, not two pools thin.
When one half has strong bankers and the other is chaos, play the strong half
straight and go wide on the chaos half.

## Per-pool construction patterns

- **Jackpot (4 legs)**: banker the shortest-priced leg, savers everywhere else. 3×3×4×4 ≈ R144 full — fractional to budget.
- **Pick 6 (6 legs)**: must include at least one confident banker or the perm explodes. 2×2×1×3×3×2 = 72 perms. Never perm full fields on a small budget — fractional down, never narrow the read.
- **Bipot (1st/2nd)**: forgiving qualifier means bankers can be looser; spend on the legs where even the first TWO are unclear.
- **PA (top-3)**: survival game — consistency (horses that hit the frame) beats brilliancy (win-or-bust types).
- **Quartet**: box the chaos (4+ horses any order) in 12+ fields with a vulnerable favourite; float 1–2 bankers to cut cost.
- **Trifecta**: Multi with a confident winner (1 for 1st, 3–4 for 2nd/3rd) in 8–13 horse fields.
- **Exacta**: duels only. Single or 2-horse box in small fields.
- **Pick 3**: three consecutive races where you have a genuine read on ALL three. 2×2×2 = R8 full. Never a lottery ticket.

## Scratchings discipline

Re-check every ticket after scratchings: a scratched banker promotes the top
saver, and a 16-horse field cut to 11 changes Quartet/Trifecta value completely.
Never run a ticket built on morning fields without a scratching pass.
