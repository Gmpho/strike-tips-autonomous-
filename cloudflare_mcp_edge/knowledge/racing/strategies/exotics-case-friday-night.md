# Case Study: Friday Night Phutulicious — Coverage vs Insight

Greyville, Friday 2 October 2026. Jackpot R6–R9, R4.80 ticket.

## The ticket

| Leg | Banker | Savers |
|-----|--------|--------|
| R6 | Anushka | Fleur De Ling, Arverni Princess, Vision To Achieve |
| R7 | Grue Of Ice | Time In Paris, Sovereign Gem, Fascination |
| R8 | Regeneration | De Vlugge, Match That, Sohot Sowhat |
| R9 | Turbo Power | Street Outlaw, My Boy Lollipop, Trippi's Silk |

## What happened

- R6: **Arverni Princess** (saver) won at 11/1, banker Anushka 2nd. Survived.
- R7: **Fascination** (saver) won, banker 3rd. Survived.
- R8: **Match That** (saver) won. Survived.
- R9: **Phutulicious** won. Not on the ticket. Dead on the last leg.

Three legs survived ON SAVERS — the construction worked three times over —
then one uncovered horse in one leg killed it.

## The twist

The engine had flagged Phutulicious in analysis that night. The read existed;
the coverage didn't. The group chat got the alert and boomed — the ticket didn't.

## The lesson (coverage vs insight)

Insight without coverage pays your friends, not you. A flagged horse left off
the ticket is a donation to the pool. Doctrine consequences, now enforced:

1. **Flagged horses auto-qualify as savers.** Any runner the analysis flags in
   a ticket leg must appear on the ticket — even if it displaces a weaker saver.
2. **Widen the murk.** R9 was the most open leg and carried only 4 coverage.
   The extra ~R1.20 of a fifth horse was the difference between a story and
   a payout.
3. **Celebrate the read, fix the process.** The engine's eye was proven right.
   The process around it is what the doctrine now hardens.
