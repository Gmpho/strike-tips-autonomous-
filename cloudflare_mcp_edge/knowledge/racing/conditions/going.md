# Going & Track Conditions in SA Racing

## Surface Types

### Turf (Grass)
Majority of SA racing. Surfaces vary by region:

- **Western Cape** (Kenilworth, Durbanville): Kikuyu/ryegrass mixes. Drainage varies — Kenilworth drains well, Fairview drains poorly.
- **Gauteng/Highveld** (Turffontein, Vaal): Summer-rainfall region, turf can go from Good to Heavy quickly during storms.
- **KZN** (Greyville, Scottsville): Subtropical climate, summer rainfall Nov-Mar. Turf can become Soft/Heavy during the season.

### Polytrack
All-weather surface used at **Fairview** (since 2013) and **Greyville** (since 2014). Wax-coated mixture of:
- Polypropylene fibres
- Recycled rubber
- Silica sand

Polytrack produces consistent times regardless of weather. Turf form generally holds up on this surface, though some horses rejuvenate their careers on it.

### Former Sand Track (Vaal)
The Vaal sand track (2001-2015) put a premium on stamina. Making up ground from behind was more difficult than on turf. Replaced by the Vaal Classic turf track in 2015/2016.

## Going Descriptions (SA Standard)

| Going | Description | Time Impact | Suitability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Good | Firm, fast surface | Normal | Most horses |
| Good to Soft | Slight give in ground | ~+0.5s per 200m | Versatile |
| Soft (Holding) | Significant penetration | ~+1.0s per 200m | Stoutly-bred |
| Heavy (Clinging) | Deep, tiring | ~+2.0s per 200m | Genuine stayers only |

## Seasonal Patterns by Region

### Western Cape (Kenilworth, Durbanville)
- **Summer (Dec-Feb):** Generally Good to firm. Cape storms can bring Soft/Heavy.
- **Winter (Jun-Aug):** Rainy season. Going often Soft/Heavy. Durbanville's excellent drainage makes it the go-to winter venue.
- **Spring/Fall:** Variable.

### Highveld (Turffontein, Vaal)
- **Summer (Nov-Mar):** Thunderstorms common. Going can change rapidly from Good to Heavy within hours.
- **Winter (May-Aug):** Dry, cold. Generally Good, sometimes firm.
- Altitude (~1700m) means faster times regardless of going.

### KZN (Greyville, Scottsville)
- **Summer (Nov-Mar):** Rainfall season. Going often Soft/Heavy. Durban July (first Saturday of July, which is winter in SA — KZN winter is mild and dry).
- **Winter (May-Aug):** Mild and dry. Generally Good going for Champions Season.

## Form Study Implications

- **Going preference:** Some horses have pronounced going preferences recorded in form. A horse with Good-to-Soft form switching to Heavy may underperform.
- **Surface switch:** Horses moving from turf to Polytrack may improve or regress — check for previous Polytrack form.
- **Altitude shift:** Horses from coast (Cape Town, Durban) moving to Highveld (Johannesburg) need adaptation time. Horses moving from Highveld to coast may struggle with the different air density.
- **Drainage:** Fairview drains slowly — going often remains Soft/Heavy for days after rain. Durbanville drains excellently.
- **Wind:** Coastal tracks (Kenilworth, Fairview, Greyville) are affected by wind. Strong winds change race dynamics significantly.
