# Newcastle Racecourse (All-Weather)

**Location:** Gosforth Park, Newcastle upon Tyne
**Surface:** Tapeta (since winter 2015/16, replaced turf flat; jumps turf kept)
**Direction:** Left-handed oval (~1m5f) + straight mile
**Going:** Standard to standard-to-slow, rain-proof but not frost-proof (lost fixtures Dec-2022)

## The defining feature

Britain's only floodlit straight mile, uphill for the final four furlongs.
Sprint trips (5f–7f) run on the straight with NO bend — ground-saving logic
imported from other AW tracks reverses here.

## Draw bias (measured, 8+ runner handicaps since 2016)

- **5f/6f/7f sprints: HIGH draws beat low** (impact ~1.2 vs ~0.85). Stands-side
  effect, no bend involved. Only quarter beating its market: top quarter over 5f.
- **Straight mile: mostly fair** (0.93/1.03/1.03); mild high lean in 11+ fields.
- **1m4f: mild high lean.** 2 miles: too thin to call.
- Use the sprint draw as a tie-breaker between liked horses, not a price edge —
  the market has eaten most of it except the top 5f quarter.

## Pace

- 5f/6f: front-runners hold an edge. 7f: edge gone. Mile: hold-up horses on top
  (uphill finish exposes early leaders). Round course: prominent racers best.

## Punter rules

- Never back a low draw here on general AW instinct — Newcastle sprints invert it.
- Stamina over the straight mile matters more than at a flat mile elsewhere.
- Course-and-distance Tapeta form is gold; turf form from elsewhere discounts.
