# Salisbury Racecourse

**Location:** Netherhampton, Wiltshire
**Surface:** Turf, straight mile (no bend) + turning extension for longer trips
**Direction:** Straight (mostly), right-handed turn for staying races

## Character

Undulating straight track — horses race up and down dips, balance matters.
No bend over the mile means no ground-saving: stalls decide position, not
trip-shortening. Big-field sprints spread across the width.

## Punter rules

- Straight-track specialists: horses that handle cambers/undulations repeat;
  flat-track gallopers can look lost.
- Draw effects are positional (which group forms) rather than rail-driven —
  read the pace map, not the stall number, in big fields.
