# Wolverhampton Racecourse (All-Weather)

**Location:** Dunstall Park, Wolverhampton
**Surface:** Tapeta (2014+; Fibresand 1993–2004, Polytrack 2004–2014 — old-surface form is VOID)
**Direction:** Left-handed oval, floodlit, tight turns

## Draw bias (Tapeta era, 8+ runner handicaps)

- **5f: LOW holds value** — one of the few sprint biases that beats its market
  (low-draw backers have shown profit to BSP/ISP). Tight turn rewards rail position.
- **6f: LOW lean**, weaker than 5f. **Mile+: largely fair**, pace dominates.
- Never import Fibresand-era "low is slow" lore — three different surfaces
  have lived here; only Tapeta figures count.

## Pace

- Sprint: prominent/forward types hold the edge; deep closers need luck
  through the turn. Stamina tests (extended mile+): pace collapses bring
  closers in.

## Punter rules

- 5f/6f: low draw + early speed is the bettable formula, and unlike most AW
  biases this one has historically paid.
- Floodlit evening cards ride the same as day — surface, not light, decides.
- Course specialists repeat; shippers from galloping turf tracks often
  underperform the market's expectation.
