# Southwell Racecourse (All-Weather)

**Location:** Rolleston, Nottinghamshire (outside NH turf course)
**Surface:** Tapeta (Dec-2021; Fibresand 1989–2021 — pre-2021 form/bias data is VOID)
**Direction:** Left-handed, flat, mile-and-a-quarter oval, two tight cambered bends

## Draw bias (Tapeta era only)

- **Straight 5f: LOW lean** (impact 1.09 vs 0.89), field-size dependent —
  real in 8–10 runner fields (1.18 vs 0.85), GONE in 11+ fields. Milder than
  the old Fibresand lottery.
- **6f/7f: level.** **Mile in 11+ fields: HIGH third wins** (1.34 vs 0.85) —
  opposite of the sprint rule. Check trip before applying any draw angle.

## Pace

- Straight 5f: strong front-running bias (A/E ~1.6, top-10 AW). 6f: edge,
  smaller. 7f: front-runners do badly. Mile+: hold-up horses on top.
- Three-furlong run-in exposes spent leaders; tight bends punish lost position.

## Punter rules

- Burn all pre-Dec-2021 Fibresand form and bias tables — different surface,
  different horses.
- Stamina counts: long straights work horses harder than tighter ovals.
  Course-and-distance Tapeta winners repeat.
- Sprint: want the lead. Staying: want the closer. Mile big-field: want high.
