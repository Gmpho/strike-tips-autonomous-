# Nottingham Racecourse

**Location:** Colwick Park, Nottinghamshire
**Surface:** Turf (flat only)
**Direction:** Left-handed, sharp, mostly flat with a slight climb late

## Character

A speed-favouring sprint track: 5f–6f races reward early position and low
draws; the turn comes fast and closers run out of room. Longer trips are
fairer — galloping types get their chance.

## Draw bias

- **5f/6f: LOW.** The turn rewards rail position; wide draws lose ground they
  never recover on the short run-in.
- **Mile+: roughly fair**, slight low favour in big fields.

## Punter rules

- Sprint handicaps: promote handy low-drawn types, demote wide closers.
- Course specialists repeat — tight-turn craft transfers poorly from
  galloping tracks.
