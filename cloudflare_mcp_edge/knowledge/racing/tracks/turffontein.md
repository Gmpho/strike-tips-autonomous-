# Turffontein Racecourse

**Location:** 14 Turf Club Street, Turffontein, Johannesburg, Gauteng
**Surface:** Turf (two tracks: Standside + Inside)
**Direction:** Right-handed (clockwise)
**Founded:** 1887 (first Johannesburg Handicap), 1892 (racecourse established)
**Operator:** 4Racing

## Standside Track (Main)

- **Circumference:** ~2600m (some sources say 2500-2800m)
- **Straight course:** 1200m — joins round course at ~800m mark
- **Regarded as the stiffest track in the country** — steady climb from ~1200m to the final turn
- Rises approximately **12m from the 1600m to the 800m mark**
- Horses racing from well off the pace hold every chance (the climb helps closers)
- Straight course is flat — the Turffontein 1200m is one of the least demanding in SA

## Inside Track (Inner)

- **Circumference:** ~2400m
- Right-handed, long sweeping bend, home straight just over 500m
- Not as steep on the final bend as the Standside track
- Draw not as important

## Draw Bias

- **Sprints (1200m):** Draw appears to be of little significance
- **1400m and 1600m:** Low barriers are favoured
- Standside track regarded as the fairest racecourse in SA overall

## History

- The first Johannesburg Handicap (forerunner of the Summer Cup) was run on 17 June 1887
- Racecourse established on the settler farm of Turffontein in 1892; grandstand foundations laid in 1893
- Night racing venue since mid-2000s (floodlights installed)
- Johannesburg Heritage Foundation Blue Plaque awarded July 2016

## Highveld Altitude

Johannesburg is at ~1700m elevation. Times are faster due to thinner air. Horses moving from coast to Highveld need an adaptation period. This is the "altitude factor" that affects KZN raiders.

## Feature Races

- **Betway Summer Cup** (Grade 1, 2160m, late Nov) — Highveld's flagship handicap
- **SA Classic** (Grade 1, 1600m, Mar) — 2nd leg of SA Triple Crown
- **SA Fillies Classic** (Grade 1, 1600m, Mar)
- **SA Derby** (Grade 2, 2450m, Apr)
- **Horse Chestnut Stakes** (Grade 1, 1600m)
- **Empress Club Stakes** (Grade 1, 1600m)
- **Premier's Champions Challenge** (Grade 1, 1600m, Apr)
- **Computaform Stakes** (Grade 1, 1600m)
