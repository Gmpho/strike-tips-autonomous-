# Fairview Racecourse (4Racing)

**Location:** Draaifontein Rd, Greenbushes, Gqeberha (Port Elizabeth), Eastern Cape
**Surface:** Turf (main) + Polytrack (all-weather inside track)
**Direction:** Right-handed (clockwise)
**Operator:** 4Racing (Pty) Ltd

## Turf Track

- **Circumference:** ~2700m near oval
- **Run-in:** 800m
- **Straight course:** 1200m — joins the round course at the 800m mark
- Straight course is **mostly downhill**, which means races are run at a true pace
- **Slow draining** — going frequently remains Soft or Heavy for some time after a wet spell
- Races up to 1200m on straight course; beyond 1200m clockwise round turn

## Draw Bias (Turf)

- **Straight course:** High number draws hold a marked advantage
- **Round turn:** Draws seem of little significance
- Horses from well off the pace have every chance

## Polytrack (All-Weather)

- **Circumference:** ~1800m
- **Run-in:** ~450m
- Right-handed, all races have at least one bend
- Opened October 2013 — first Polytrack in South Africa
- Low numbers and/or early speed may count as advantage, especially over 1000m
- Near bend leading into home straight has a slight camber to assist horses

## History

- Racing in PE dates to 1857 (Port Elizabeth Turf Club formed)
- Jockey Club of South Africa founded in PE in 1882
- Old Fairview was where Greenacres Shopping Centre is today
- Current track at Greenbushes opened in 1977

## Feature Races

- **Algoa Cup** (signature day)
- **Port Elizabeth Gold Cup** (3200m stayer's feature)
- Fairview Twilight racing (Friday evenings, year-round)
