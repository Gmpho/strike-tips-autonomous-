# Hollywoodbets Greyville Racecourse

**Location:** 150 Avondale Rd, Greyville, Durban, KZN
**Surface:** Turf (main) + Polytrack (all-weather, installed 2014)
**Direction:** Right-handed (clockwise)
**Turf circumference:** 2800m pear-shaped
**Turf run-in:** ~450m (nearly level final straight)
**Founded:** 1844 (first meeting), 1896 (course built)
**Operator:** Gold Circle / Hollywoodbets
**Capacity:** 55,000

## Turf Track Gradients

The construction of two subways (1929) created unusual gradient features:

1. **Uphill:** from 2200m mark to 1800m mark
2. **Gentle downward slope:** from ~1800m to ~1200m mark
3. **Uphill again:** from 1000m mark into the straight
4. **Almost level:** 450m final straight to the winning post

This makes Greyville a very testing ride.

## Polytrack (All-Weather)

- 2000m circumference, positioned inside the turf track
- Maximum field: 12 runners (initially limited to 10 for distances 1000-1600m)
- Same run-in of ~450m
- All races run around the turn
- Opened 11 June 2014 — first Polytrack in KZN
- Racing every Friday night from September to April
- Turf form generally holds up on this surface

## Draw Bias

- **Inside draws** generally favoured up to 1900m, especially 1600m (start is right on the top bend)
- Wide draws can be negated by false rail position
- Horses making their run on the outside hold advantage when going is soft
- **Polytrack:** Low draws preferred; ability to race handy appears even more important than on turf
- Strong south-westerly tailwind helps front-runners

## History

- First horse racing meeting in KZN was July 1844, close to the site of the present course
- **Vodacom Durban July** first run in 1897 (7 runners, won by Campanajo)
- Queen Elizabeth II and Prince Philip visited in 1995
- King George VI, Queen Elizabeth, Princesses Elizabeth and Margaret visited in 1947
- **First floodlit track in SA** (1996)
- **First Sunday meeting in SA** (February 1996)
- Infield holds Royal Durban Golf Club's championship course
- Renamed Hollywoodbets Greyville in 2019 (3-year naming rights deal)

## Feature Races (Champions Season)

- **Hollywoodbets Durban July** (Grade 1, 2200m, first Saturday of July) — R10M total stakes (2026)
- **Garden Province Stakes** (Grade 1, 1600m)
- **Daily News 2000** (Grade 1, 2000m)
- **Woolavington 2000** (Grade 1, 2000m)
- **Gold Challenge** (Grade 1, 1600m)
- **Gold Cup** (Grade 3, 3000m)

Greyville hosts 12 Grade 1 races during Champions Season with over R44M total stakes.
