# Hollywoodbets Kenilworth Racecourse

**Location:** Rosmead Ave, Kenilworth, Cape Town, Western Cape
**Surface:** Turf
**Direction:** Left-handed (anti-clockwise)
**Founded:** 1881 (oldest racecourse in South Africa)
**Operator:** Cape Racing (Kenilworth Racing PTY LTD)

## Tracks

Kenilworth is unique in SA — three separate tracks that converge only in the pull-up area:

- **New Course:** 2800m circumference, ~600m run-in. Used primarily in summer (Dec-Feb). One of the fairest tracks in SA — gives every horse a winning chance.
- **Old Course:** 2700m circumference, ~450m run-in. Used primarily for winter racing. Ability to race handy is more important here.
- **Straight Course:** 1200m straight that bisects the infield diagonally. Regarded as the stiffest 1200m in the country — climbs for the first 200m and again over the final 200m. A real test of ability to stay 1200m; horses that go fast early often get caught at the finish.

## Draw Bias

- **Sprints (1200m):** Low number draws generally favoured. Fields split into two groups when going is heavy — outside barriers then enjoy a slight advantage.
- **Round turn:** Draws are of little importance, especially on the new course.
- **Wet going:** Can impact draw in races down the straight.

## Going Impact

Cape Town's winter (Jun-Aug) brings rain — going frequently Soft to Heavy. Summer (Dec-Feb) is typically Good. Cape storms can cause Soft/Heavy even in summer.

## Feature Races

- **Sun Met** (Grade 1, 2000m, late Jan) — SA's most prestigious race
- **Cape Guineas** (Grade 1, 1600m, Dec)
- **Cape Fillies Guineas** (Grade 1, 1600m, Dec)
- **L'Ormarins King's Plate** (Grade 1, 1600m, Jan)
- **Cape Derby** (Grade 1, 2000m, Feb)
- **Majorca Stakes** (Grade 1, 1600m, Jan)

Most Grade 1 action at Kenilworth takes place in December and January (Summer Season).
