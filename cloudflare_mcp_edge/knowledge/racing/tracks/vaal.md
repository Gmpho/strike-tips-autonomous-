# Vaal Racecourse

**Location:** 1 Ascot on Vaal, Viljoensdrif, Gauteng
**Surface:** Turf (two tracks: Vaal Classic + Vaal Turf)
**Direction:** Right-handed (clockwise)
**Distance from Jo'burg:** ~70km south
**Founded:** 1946 (formal registration); sand track added 2001, replaced with turf in 2015

## Vaal Classic Track

- **Circumference:** 2800m oval
- **Run-in:** 1000m (the longest in SA)
- Races up to 1000m run straight; beyond 1000m clockwise round turn
- 1200m races start in the middle of the bend, 1450m at the entrance of the bend

## Vaal Turf Track

- **Circumference:** 3000m oval (longest in SA)
- **Run-in:** 1000m
- **Straight course:** 1600m — the longest straight in South Africa
- Races up to 1600m run straight; beyond 1600m clockwise round turn

## Draw Bias

- **Vaal Classic (1200m, 1450m):** Low-number draws favoured
- **Vaal Turf (1800m):** Low-number draws slightly favoured
- **1000m:** High number draws favoured
- **Straight course in winter:** Fields tend to split into two groups on either side of the course — both extremes appear equally favourable
- Despite the long run-in, horses racing handy or in front frequently do well
- Draws are of little significance in races around the turn

## Former Sand Track

- Right-handed with 1000m straight course
- Sand track put a premium on stamina — making up ground from behind was more difficult than on turf
- Inaugurated August 2001, closed 2015, replaced by Vaal Classic turf track (2016)

## Feature Races

- **World Sports Betting Grand Heritage** (1475m straight, 28-runner cavalry charge)
- **Emerald Cup** (formerly signature sand race — no longer run)

## Key Characteristics

- Two 1000m straights connected by fairly sharp bends
- The Vaal is the "survivor track" — born outside the glamour circuit, rebuilt around whatever the industry needed next
- Country racing atmosphere — relaxed, family-friendly
- Served as the Vaal Training Centre base
