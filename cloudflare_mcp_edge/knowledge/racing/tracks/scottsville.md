# Hollywoodbets Scottsville Racecourse

**Location:** 45 New England Rd, Scottsville, Pietermaritzburg, KZN
**Surface:** Turf
**Direction:** Right-handed (clockwise)
**Circumference:** ~2300m oval
**Run-in:** 550-600m (all of it up a fairly steady incline)
**Founded:** 1886 (as City Sporting Club)
**Operator:** Gold Circle / Hollywoodbets (since 2019 naming rights)

## Track Configuration

- Right-handed oval with a **banked turn** allowing easy transition into the home straight
- **1200m straight course** with a moveable rail for interchangeable run-in length
- The straight course runs downhill for the first 400m, but uphill for the last 500m
- 1200m at Scottsville is a testing distance — ability to see out the distance is essential
- With a downhill run to ~600m mark and a climb to the finish, races are usually run at a true pace — horses who go too fast early are found out by the climb to the finish

## Draw Bias

- **Sprints:** Horses from off the pace usually do well
- **Soft going:** High numbers are favoured on the straight course
- **1600m and up:** Barrier draws seem of little importance
- Inside and outside tracks for races beyond 1200m (common back straight and turns)

## Features

- Adjacent to the **Golden Horse Casino** (22 tables, 450 slot machines)
- The 14m Pegasus statue at the rear is the largest horse statue in the world
- Setting for the 2005 film *Racing Stripes* (about a racing zebra)
- An enclosed picnic area available

## Feature Races (May)

- **Golden Horse Sprint** (Grade 1, 1200m)
- **SA Fillies Sprint** (Grade 1, 1200m)
- **Gold Medallion** (Grade 1, 1200m)
- **Allan Robertson Championship** (Grade 1, 1200m)

Four Grade 1 races on the same day in May — one of the biggest race days in SA.

## History

- First sponsored race in SA history: Gilbey's Stakes (1962) hosted at Scottsville
- Smirnoff Plate (1963) also hosted here — both absorbed into the Golden Horse Sprint in 1996
- Course was originally maintained by plough and oxen (old-fashioned method)
- Local bikers with belt-propelled bikes were once asked to ride on the track to disperse divots
